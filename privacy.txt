# SERVERSTARTER Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SERVERSTARTER ("the Bot") handles information when you use it within Discord.

## 2. Information Collected

SERVERSTARTER does **not** intentionally collect or permanently store personal information.

The Bot may temporarily process information provided by the Discord API in order to respond to commands or perform requested actions. This may include:

* Discord User IDs
* Discord Server (Guild) IDs
* Discord Channel IDs
* Discord Role IDs
* Discord usernames or display names, when required to execute a command

This information is processed only as necessary for the Bot to function.

## 3. Data Storage

SERVERSTARTER does not permanently store user data.

Any information processed while handling commands exists only for the duration necessary to complete the requested action.

## 4. Data Sharing

SERVERSTARTER does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The Bot communicates only with Discord through the official Discord API to provide its functionality.

## 5. Third-Party Services

SERVERSTARTER relies on the Discord API to operate. Your use of Discord is governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to help protect the Bot and its systems. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy

SERVERSTARTER is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements. The Bot is not directed toward children.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will become effective when the revised version is published.
